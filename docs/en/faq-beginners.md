# Beginner FAQ

[Version française](../fr/faq-debutants.md) · [Glossary](glossary-terms.md)

Answers concern this study path. The [source matrix](../../sources/source-matrix.md) and [uncertainty register](../uncertainty-register.md), read here on 2026-10-18, retain the dates of their underlying checks; this FAQ does not revalidate those external sources.

## 1. Is this official?

No. This repository is independent community material, with no affiliation, endorsement or certification claimed. Its scope is set out in the [notice](../../NOTICE.md).

## 2. Where should I start?

Start with [Module 0](00-discovering-oda.md), then follow the prerequisites at the top of each module. Keep the [glossary](glossary-terms.md) nearby when a word gets in the way of the example.

## 3. What does the verified part contain?

The Checked section of the [source matrix](../../sources/source-matrix.md#checked) records specific statements, sources, reading dates and limits. It does not mean that every module statement, full specification or runtime behaviour has been independently validated.

## 4. Why use invented names?

Names such as plan agreement and connection workshop let us explain meaning and responsibility without presenting our exercise as a standard model. [Module 2](02-sid-in-practice.md#worked-example) explicitly keeps these objects separate from official names and attributes.

## 5. Can I pass a certification with this path?

The path helps you practise reasoning about its own examples; it awards no certification and predicts no exam result. [Module 7](07-preparing-for-certification.md#limits-of-this-module) explains that limit; use the dated [matrix](../../sources/source-matrix.md) and open questions U3 and U6 in the [register](../uncertainty-register.md) before drawing conclusions about your own exam situation.

## 6. Are the quiz questions exam questions?

No. They were written for our examples and point back to our module sections. The [self-test lab](../../labs/07-self-test/README.md) supports explanation and rereading, without an exam score or a claim of exam coverage.

## 7. Does a passing lab prove conformance?

It proves only what that local check observed. For example, [Module 5](05-components-in-practice.md#limits-of-this-module) limits its tests to input and dependency rules, with no real-system interoperability or conformance claim.

## 8. Do I need a running operator platform?

The [labs index](../../labs/README.md) distinguishes documented work from locally executed exercises and gives each lab’s requirements. Begin with the synthetic examples; a proposed architecture in [Module 8](08-capstone-architecture-review.md#limits-of-this-module) is not a deployed operator system.

## 9. Is the current SID version settled here?

No current-version conclusion is made here. U1 remains partial in the [uncertainty register](../uncertainty-register.md); read the dated evidence and its limits in the [matrix](../../sources/source-matrix.md) instead of treating a visible catalogue number as a complete verification.

## 10. Has TAM been replaced?

This path does not settle that question. U5 remains partial in the [register](../uncertainty-register.md), and [Module 3](03-functional-framework-in-practice.md#where-tam-fits) states no TAM status or mapping to the Functional Framework.

## 11. Can I use real customer records in the labs?

Use synthetic records only. The [Module 4 exercise](04-open-apis-in-practice.md#exercise) asks for invented fields and references; none of these learning tasks needs a real customer identifier or telephone number.

## 12. What should I do when a source disagrees with a page?

Record the page, the precise statement, the public source URL and the date you read it, then propose a correction as described in the [README](../../README.md#contributing). Keep the doubt visible in the [register](../uncertainty-register.md) until the evidence supports an update to the [matrix](../../sources/source-matrix.md).
